# Strategic Business Recommendations & Action Framework

## Structure & Evidence Classification

To maintain rigorous professional standards, this document categorizes recommendations into three explicit evidentiary tiers:
1. **Tier 1: Direct Evidence** — Actions immediately justified and validated by empirical numbers in the dataset.
2. **Tier 2: Plausible Hypotheses** — Strong operational deductions that require cross-functional validation with sales and supply chain teams.
3. **Tier 3: Future Data Requirements** — Strategic initiatives that require external data collection (e.g., freight cost structures, customer acquisition costs, return rates).

---

## Tier 1: Directly Supported by Data (Immediate Implementation)

### 1. Hard Cap on Commercial Discounts at 20%
* **Empirical Evidence**:
  - Transactions discounted <= 20% generate an aggregate **+$447,755** in operating profit (average margin 18.2%).
  - Transactions discounted > 20% generated an aggregate loss of **-$161,359**.
  - Transactions discounted > 30% produced a **0% profitable transaction rate** (all 712 transactions were loss-makers).
* **Execution**:
  - Configure the sales order management system (ERP / CRM) with a hard discount limit of 20%.
  - Any request above 20% requires formal Vice President sign-off with documented margin impact.

### 2. Elimination of Discounts on High-Loss Technology SKUs
* **Empirical Evidence**:
  - Three 3D printers and laser printers (`TEC-MA-10000418`, `TEC-MA-10000822`, `TEC-MA-10004125`) generated $35,929.82 in revenue but wiped out **-$17,309.93 in profit** (-48.2% average margin) due to average discounts between 40% and 53%.
* **Execution**:
  - Remove all promotional discount eligibility for these three product IDs immediately.
  - Set a minimum floor price guaranteeing at least 15% net margin.

### 3. Restructuring Furniture & Tables Pricing
* **Empirical Evidence**:
  - Furniture delivers $741,999.73 in revenue (32.3% of total company sales) but only $18,451.10 in profit (2.49% margin).
  - The Tables sub-category alone lost **-$17,725.57** (-8.56% margin).
* **Execution**:
  - Lower the maximum allowable discount on Tables from 40% to 15%.
  - Require bundling of Tables with high-margin accessories or office chairs to achieve positive basket profitability.

### 4. Regional Intervention in Central Territory & Loss States
* **Empirical Evidence**:
  - The Central region's profit margin (7.92%) is nearly half that of the West (14.94%), driven by an excessive 24.04% average discount.
  - Four states (Texas -$25.7K, Ohio -$17.0K, Pennsylvania -$15.6K, Illinois -$12.6K) erased over **$70,000** of enterprise value.
* **Execution**:
  - Realign regional sales incentives in the Central territory from gross revenue volume to net margin contribution.
  - Review all distributor agreements in Texas and Ohio.

## Tier 2: Plausible Hypotheses (Requiring Cross-Functional Validation)

### 1. Freight & Bulky Item Shipping Subsidies
* **Hypothesis**: The severe losses in Tables and Bookcases are heavily compounded by free or subsidized shipping of heavy, oversized items across long transit distances.
* **Validation Step**: Convene with the Logistics & Fulfillment Director to review carrier weight surcharges and regional freight tariffs on furniture SKUs.
* **Potential Action**: Introduce a localized freight surcharge on all shipments exceeding 50 lbs or requiring two-person delivery.

### 2. High-Value Customer "Gaming" of Discount Rules
* **Hypothesis**: High-spend buyers (such as Sean Miller, -$1,980.74 profit on $25,043.05 spend) are taking advantage of uncapped end-of-quarter sales rep discount flexibility on expensive hardware.
* **Validation Step**: Audit sales rep commission structures to determine if commissions were paid on revenue rather than gross profit.
* **Potential Action**: Transition sales commission plans to gross margin percentages rather than top-line dollar volume.

## Tier 3: Actions Requiring Additional Data Collection

### 1. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) and Retention Lifetime Value (LTV)
* **Required Data**: Marketing spend per channel (Google Ads, Email, Direct Sales), lead source attribution, and churn rates.
* **Objective**: Measure true customer ROI across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments.

### 2. Product Return & Damage Rates
* **Required Data**: Return authorization logs, restocking fees, and salvage values.
* **Objective**: Determine whether returns in Furniture are even higher than indicated by the initial transactional records.

### 3. Competitor Price Monitoring
* **Required Data**: Scraping or syndication of competitor prices for identical office technology and furniture SKUs.
* **Objective**: Ensure that capping discounts at 20% does not result in unexpected volume loss to competitors who maintain lower baseline list prices.
