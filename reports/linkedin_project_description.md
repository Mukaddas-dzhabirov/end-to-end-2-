# LinkedIn Portfolio Project Description

## Project Title
**End-to-End Sales & Business Performance Analytics: Transforming Retail Data into Actionable Business Insights Using SQL, Python, and Power BI**

---

## Concise Project Description
Designed and engineered a complete end-to-end commercial analytics solution analyzing 9,994 retail sales transactions across the United States. Built an automated data cleaning and validation pipeline in Python, developed advanced analytical SQL scripts utilizing CTEs and window functions, architected an enterprise Star Schema data model with 20+ DAX measures, and created a 3-page interactive Power BI dashboard to diagnose revenue growth, customer concentration, and profit margin erosion.

---

## Skills Demonstrated
* **Advanced SQL**: Window functions (`LAG`, `DENSE_RANK`, `ROW_NUMBER`, `NTILE`), Common Table Expressions (CTEs), multi-table relational joins, conditional aggregation, and running totals.
* **Python Engineering**: Data wrangling (`pandas`, `numpy`), data quality validation framework, automated feature engineering, and unit testing (`pytest`).
* **Business Intelligence & Modeling**: Ralph Kimball Star Schema data modeling, relationship cardinality optimization, and Date dimension setup.
* **DAX & Power BI**: 20+ custom DAX measures for executive KPIs, time intelligence (YoY, YTD, MoM), and visual design.
* **Commercial Business Acumen**: Margin leakage identification, discount elasticity analysis, regional performance auditing, and executive reporting.

---

## Measurable Project Deliverables & Empirical Findings
1. **Automated ETL & Quality Audit Pipeline**: Cleaned, standardized, and validated 9,994 records with 0 duplicate rows and 100% chronological date consistency, exporting star schema dimensions (`DimCustomer`, `DimProduct`, `DimGeography`, `DimDate`, `FactSales`).
2. **Identification of $85,000+ Margin Leakage**: Diagnosed a severe commercial "Profit Cliff" at the 20% discount threshold; proved transactions with discounts above 20% produced aggregate losses of -$161,359, with >30% discount tiers yielding a 0% profitable transaction rate.
3. **Divisional Diagnostic & Furniture Turnaround Plan**: Uncovered that while Technology (17.4% margin) and Office Supplies (17.0% margin) drove strong profits, Furniture delivered $742K in sales but only 2.49% profit margin, primarily drained by Tables (-$17,725 loss) and Bookcases (-$3,472 loss).
4. **Regional Optimization Strategy**: Isolated that the Central Region underperformed (7.92% margin) compared to the West (14.94% margin) due to an elevated average discount rate of 24.04%, pinpointing $70,000+ in accumulated losses across Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania, and Illinois.
5. **Interactive 3-Page Executive Dashboard**: Designed full wireframes, KPI cards, interactive slicers, and DAX formula library across Executive Overview, Product & Regional Performance, and Customer Analytics.
